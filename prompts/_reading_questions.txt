[core]
## Questions for Core Reading:
- What seems to have begun, ended, or shifted in this section? Did a new scene, act, or argumentative turn start partway through?
- What changed in stakes, goals, leverage, or power balance here?
- Who or what is central in this section? Are these returning elements or newly introduced ones?
- What newly established, revised, or contradicted facts matter going forward?
- What is the single most load-bearing sentence, image, beat, or claim in this section?
- What feels unresolved now, and what is the most urgent next question?

[meta]
## Meta / Session overlay:
- What do I currently think this piece is trying to do?
- What am I uncertain about but willing to keep watching?
- What is my current confidence in my interpretation?
- Have any earlier expectations been confirmed or overturned?
- Am I more or less engaged than I was one section ago?

[reader_experience]
## Reader Experience overlay, for enjoyment and engagement:
- What emotional temperature did this section carry?
- Was it interesting, boring, gripping, funny, moving, tense, or flat? Why?
- Did the pacing accelerate, stall, or pivot?
- What moment, line, image, or exchange landed best for you?
- What do you most want to see next?
- What do you predict will happen next, and how confident are you?

[narrative]
## Narrative / Storycraft overlay:
- Which characters are active here, and how did their relationships or goals change?
- Did any character arrive, depart, transform, resolve, or die in a way that feels consequential?
- Did the setting, tone, POV, style, or dramatic focus shift?
- Is this section doing setup, escalation, complication, climax, fallout, or transition?
- Does anything here feel like foreshadowing, payoff, misdirection, or thematic reinforcement?
- Is the plot development satisfying so far?

[developmental_edit]
## Developmental Editing overlay:
- Where does the piece feel unclear, under-motivated, redundant, rushed, or over-explained?
- Are there implied scene breaks or section breaks that the draft has not marked clearly?
- What would an editor want to verify, move, trim, expand, or reorder?
- Does the emotional or logical progression feel earned?
- Are there continuity issues, tonal clashes, dangling threads, voice shifts, or perspective problems?
- What is the highest-value revision opportunity in this section?

[argument]
## Argument / Rhetoric overlay:
- What claim, premise, frame, or worldview is being advanced here?
- What assumptions or axioms are being laid down or smuggled in?
- What evidence or reasoning is offered, and how strong does it seem?
- Did the argument shift, narrow, widen, or contradict itself?
- What is the strongest point here? What is the weakest?
- What would a skeptical reader push back on next?

[worldbuilding]
## Worldbuilding / Setting overlay:
- What new facts about the world, system, or context were established here?
- What norms, rules, institutions, or constraints became clearer?
- Did anything contradict prior expectations about the setting or framework?
- What feels suggestive but not yet fully explained?
- What detail most effectively made the world feel real?

[style]
## Style / Language overlay:
- What stylistic choices stand out here?
- What metaphors, images, dialogue, or phrasing are especially effective?
- Does the voice feel stable, evolving, or inconsistent?
- Is the prose working with the content or fighting it?
- What sentence or paragraph best represents how this piece sounds?

[scientific_research]
## Scientific/Research Analysis overlay:
- What is the main research question or hypothesis in this section?
- What claim is actually being made here, and how narrow or broad is it?
- What methods, data, or experimental design are being used, and do they seem appropriate to the question?
- What assumptions, controls, or limitations matter most here?
- Are the results being described clearly, or is the prose smuggling interpretation in as if it were fact?
- Do the conclusions seem supported by the evidence presented in this section?
- What would a skeptical but fair reader want clarified, replicated, or tested further?
- Is there any place where correlation, speculation, or weak inference is being presented too confidently?
- What is the most important technical finding or takeaway here?
- Are there are any lurking biases, faulty methodologies, or plausible fabrication of data?
- What remains uncertain, unresolved, or clearly outside the scope of the study?

[technical_systems]
## Technical/Systems overlay:
- What system, component, or process is being described here?
- What problem is this section trying to solve, and for whom?
- Are the requirements, inputs, outputs, and constraints clear?
- What dependencies, assumptions, or prerequisites are implied or left unstated?
- Does the explanation match how a real user, operator, or implementer would think about the task?
- Are there edge cases, failure modes, or operational risks that should be called out more explicitly?
- What tradeoffs are being made here, and are they justified?
- Is anything hand-waved that would matter in real implementation, maintenance, scaling, security, or debugging?
- What is the most useful or load-bearing concept in this section?
- What would a competent engineer, admin, or user still need to know after reading this?
